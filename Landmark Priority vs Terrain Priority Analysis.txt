===============================================================================
LANDMARK PRIORITY vs TERRAIN PRIORITY -- WHICH ORDER DRIVES BETTER?
===============================================================================
Written in plain English, same as the pre-demo debugging file.

THE QUESTION
------------
The car has two "opinion givers" that can disagree:

  - the TERRAIN layer (vision/terrain.py) looks at the ground and says
    "that is grass, do NOT drive there";
  - the LANDMARK layer (Landmarks.py) recognises a place and says
    "you are at the turn spot, now go LEFT and UP the grassy slope".

On a grassy hill these two fight: terrain wants to avoid the grass, but the
whole point of the turn is to climb that grassy slope. So who should win? This
note walks through both answers and picks one.

FIRST, WHAT NEVER CHANGES
-------------------------
No matter which order you choose, the ULTRASONIC REFLEX always wins over both.
"Don't hit a wall" is never overruled by a landmark or by the terrain. So this
whole discussion is only about grass-vs-place, never about crashing.

Also remember the terrain layer is not "any green = stop". It treats grass as a
DENSITY. The mud course is about a third grass tufts and still counts as
drivable. Only a patch that is mostly grass (over ~55%) is a keep-out. The
grassy HILL is mostly grass, so that is where the fight actually happens.


ORDER A -- TERRAIN PRIORITY  (grass keep-out can overrule the landmark)
======================================================================
This is the cautious order. A landmark can only ADVANCE THE PLAN; it cannot
force the wheels onto grass. If the terrain says "mostly grass ahead", the car
steers away or stops, even mid-turn.

How the car drives:
  + On the mud course and the flat sections it is very safe. It will not wander
    onto the manicured lawn or climb a bank by mistake.
  + Surface changes (mud -> gravel -> cement) still drive the route fine.
  - On the grassy hill it FIGHTS ITSELF. The turn sequence says "go up the
    slope", but the slope is mostly grass, so the terrain keep-out refuses and
    steers the car back down or sideways. The climb stalls, the "rotate until
    View" step never gets a chance, and the turn looks broken.

In one line: SAFE everywhere, but it cannot finish the one manoeuvre that
REQUIRES driving on grass. For this course, that is a mission failure.


ORDER B -- LANDMARK PRIORITY  (the place beats the grass keep-out)
=================================================================
This is the bold order. When a landmark says "do the turn", the car does it and
ignores the grass keep-out for that manoeuvre.

How the car drives:
  + The grassy-hill turn becomes reliable. The car commits: pivot left, climb
    the grass to the crest, rotate right until it sees the View. This is exactly
    the behaviour you want at that spot.
  - But the keep-out is the thing that KEPT THE CAR OFF the lawn and the wrong
    banks. Weaken it and the car drives over grass more freely. If a landmark is
    mis-recognised (and phone-photo references will mis-fire sometimes), or if
    the priority is on during ordinary driving, the car can happily trundle onto
    the lawn it was supposed to avoid.

In one line: the turns work, but the car is grass-happy and can wander where it
shouldn't -- more foolproof turns, buggier everywhere else.


THE MIDDLE GROUND (what Landmarks.py already does, and the recommendation)
=========================================================================
Neither extreme is right. Pure terrain priority can't finish the route; pure
landmark priority is reckless on grass. The fix is to make the priority DEPEND
ON WHAT THE CAR IS DOING right now:

  - While just FOLLOWING the ground between places  -> TERRAIN wins (stay off
    grass, don't wander).
  - While doing a NAMED MANOEUVRE that needs it (climb the slope, cross the
    step, rotate to the View) -> the LANDMARK/plan wins for the length of that
    manoeuvre only.
  - The ultrasonic reflex wins over both, always.

This is already how Landmarks.py is built: in its "follow" phases it asks the
terrain layer where to steer (terrain priority), and only during the climb /
turn / cross phases does it drive directly and ignore the grass (landmark
priority). So you get the reliable grassy-hill turn WITHOUT giving the car
permission to drive on grass the rest of the time.

Two guard rails keep the bold part safe:
  - a place must be seen for several frames before a manoeuvre starts, so a
    single mis-read cannot send the car up a random grass bank;
  - each manoeuvre has a time limit, so if something is wrong the car falls back
    to ordinary, terrain-respecting driving instead of grinding up grass forever.

WHICH IS MORE FOOLPROOF?
------------------------
  - Pure TERRAIN priority: safest against wandering, but FAILS THE ROUTE (can't
    climb the grassy hill). Not foolproof for this course.
  - Pure LANDMARK priority: finishes the turns, but is buggy and can drive onto
    the lawn. Not foolproof either.
  - SCOPED priority (terrain by default, landmark only during the manoeuvres
    that need it, reflex always on): gets the reliable turn AND keeps the car
    off grass the rest of the time. THIS is the most foolproof, and it is the
    default already in Landmarks.py.

HOW TO SHIFT THE BALANCE IF YOU WANT TO
---------------------------------------
  - Want it BOLDER (even more foolproof turns, at the cost of more grass
    driving): let the climb phase ignore terrain entirely (it already does) and
    lengthen the climb time limit so a slow, grass-heavy climb isn't cut short.
  - Want it SAFER (less grass driving, at the risk of a stalled turn): shorten
    the manoeuvre time limits and raise the number of confirmation frames, so
    the car only commits to a climb when it is very sure of the place.
  - The one thing NOT to change: never let a landmark override the ultrasonic
    reflex. Foolproof turning is not worth a crash.
